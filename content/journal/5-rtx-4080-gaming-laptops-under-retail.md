The 50-series already took the launch-window tax. What is left on the 4080 board is not last-gen junk. It is 12GB of Ada, 7424 CUDA cores, and chassis that originally printed between roughly $2,500 and $3,800. In September 2026 those same SKUs are the liquid mid-book: enough TGP to hold 1440p/1600p ultra, enough VRAM that you are not swapping textures in 2026 titles, and enough seller volume that you can actually exit.

This is not a spec sheet. It is a BIN board. We ranked five RTX 4080 laptops that still trade under their original U.S. list — the ones with real two-way flow on eBay, not boutique one-offs. Pay for a complete kit (charger, battery cycle honesty, no hinge crack). Walk from “for parts,” battery-swollen Razers, and any listing that will not show GPU-Z TGP or a HWInfo screenshot.

**Rule: original retail is the ceiling, not the bid.** If the ask is within 10% of 2023/2024 list, you are funding someone else’s 50-series upgrade.

## How we graded the board

- **Spread to retail** — original U.S. list vs what complete units have actually been clearing used/refurb.
- **TGP honesty** — 175W Scar/Legion/Vector/Blade vs ~115W Zephyrus. Same GPU name, not the same chip in practice.
- **Two-way liquidity** — can you sell it in 14 days without eating the bid.
- **Condition tells** — coil whine, hinge, liquid metal on Blade/Scar, 16GB RAM configs that look cheap until you price a kit.

## 1. Lenovo Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 (16IRX9) — the index

This is the chassis the board actually uses as a price. Original U.S. configs sat around **$2,999–$3,219** for i9-14900HX + RTX 4080 + 32GB + 1TB/2TB + 16" 2560×1600 240Hz. Manufacturer-refurbished units have been printing in the **high $1,400s to low $1,900s** through 2026. That is not a coupon. That is a 35–50% haircut to list, with a 175W-class 4080 that already holds QHD.

Buy the 32GB SKU. The 16GB Pro 7i looks like a steal until you eat a kit plus downtime. Confirm it is **16IRX9 / 83DE**, not a Pro 5 or Slim 7 with a 4070 resticker. Ask for Legion Vantage screenshots: GPU TGP, MUX on, and battery cycle count. Cold paste and a toasted vapor chamber are the two ways a “great deal” becomes a $200 teardown.

Liquidity is the point. A complete [used Legion Pro 7i Gen 9 with RTX 4080](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=%22Legion+Pro+7i%22+%22RTX+4080%22+-parts+-broken+-cracked) turns faster than a Scar and cheaper than a Blade. If you are flipping, this is the unit you can bid, grade, and relist without explaining RGB mythology to the next buyer.

### Pass if

- Seller will not photo the underside vents or the 330W brick.
- RAM is soldered-plus-slot and they will not confirm total capacity in Task Manager.
- The panel is 1080p. The Pro 7i 4080 you want is the 1600p 240Hz, not a stripped panel.

## 2. MSI Vector 16 HX — the floor BIN

MSI never got the cultural bid the Scar and Blade get. That is why it is on this list. The Vector 16 HX (A13VHG / A14VHG) launched in the **$2,499–$2,699** band for i9 + 4080 + 32GB. Street comps have printed under $2,000 in the 50-series dump window; MSI’s own U.S. store has shown the A13VHG-464US 4080 at **$1,699** against a $2,199 strike. You are buying a 175W 4080 and a SteelSeries keyboard, not a flex object.

The Vector’s tell is the panel. A lot of A13 units shipped **1920×1200 144Hz**. Fine for 1080p esports, a tax if you thought you bought a mini-LED Scar. Filter for QHD+ 240Hz A14V configs when the spread is still wide. 32GB DDR5 is table stakes; 16GB is a pass unless the BIN is so low you can kit it and still sit under $1,800 all-in.

Condition: Cooler Boost 5 is loud and that is normal. What is not normal is a grinding fan or a board that thermal-throttles at 70C because someone ran it on a bed for two years. Demand a Cinebench + Time Spy screenshot, or walk. Hunt a [complete MSI Vector 16 HX RTX 4080 with 32GB](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=%22Vector+16+HX%22+4080+32GB+-parts+-broken+-cracked) — “complete” meaning 330W adapter, not a 240W Dell brick in the photo.

## 3. ASUS ROG Strix Scar 16 (2024) G634 — max TGP, min patience

This is the 175W 4080 with the Nebula mini-LED: 16" 2560×1600, 240Hz, 1100 nits on the HDR SKU. Original U.S. list on the G634JZR-XS96 sat around **$3,399–$3,500**. Price trackers have printed a low of **$1,649.99**. That low is a lightning deal, not the bid — treat **low-to-mid $2,000s** for a clean 32GB/1TB as the under-retail zone, and anything near $3,300 as a seller who did not get the memo that 50-series exists.

You pay Scar money for MUX, high TGP, and a panel that actually uses the 4080. You do not pay Scar money for a yellowed WASD cluster, a missing keycap, or liquid metal that migrated because the laptop lived vertically in a backpack. ASUS liquid metal is the condition exam: ask for a close-up of the CPU/GPU cold plate if they already opened it, and a 30-minute Time Spy run if they did not.

Model discipline: **G634JZ / G634JZR / G634JYR** for 2024 Scar 16 4080. G16 Strix (G614) is a different thermal and a different bid. Search [ROG Strix Scar 16 G634 with RTX 4080](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=%22Strix+Scar+16%22+%28G634%2C+G634JZ%2C+G634JZR%29+4080+-parts+-broken) and exclude 4070/4090 so you are not averaging three GPUs into one price.

### Pass if

- It is a Scar 18 G834 being sold as a 16. Different weight, different bid.
- The listing is “new open box” from a seller with no returns and a stock photo.
- RAM is 16GB. On a Scar 16 that is a configuration you kit or skip.

## 4. ASUS ROG Zephyrus G16 (2024) GU605 — the travel 4080

Same GPU name, not the same product. The 2024 Zephyrus G16 (GU605MZ) pairs Core Ultra 9 185H with an RTX 4080 at about **115W TGP** and a 240Hz QHD OLED, soldered 32GB, ~1.85kg. Original U.S. list sat near **$2,699**. Best Buy had it at **$2,299–$2,399** in the first dump; used/good comps still print under that list. You buy this when you actually carry the laptop, not when you want Scar scores.

The bid exists because OLED + thin is a different buyer than the Legion crowd. Do not mix comps. A Zephyrus 4080 that “loses” a Time Spy to a Vector is not a bad unit — it is a 60W TGP gap. Grade burn-in on the OLED (taskbar ghosting, desktop icon shadows) the way you grade a Submariner bezel. No charger in the photos is a walk; the GaN brick is part of the kit value.

Soldered RAM means the 32GB SKU is the only SKU. Filter [Zephyrus G16 GU605 RTX 4080 32GB OLED](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=%22Zephyrus+G16%22+%28GU605%2C+GU605MZ%29+4080+-parts+-broken+-4070) and kill 4070 results — the 4070 G16 is a different ceiling and a different floor.

## 5. Razer Blade 16 (2024) RZ09-0510 — chassis tax, finally marked down

Razer sold the 4080 Blade 16 like it was a Royal Oak. Original U.S. tags on 32GB/2TB 4080 configs sat **$3,399–$3,799** (Mercury dual mini-LED and OLED both printed in that band). That was never the fair bid. It was the CNC tax. In 2026 the 50-series Blade and every 175W 4080 tower on eBay finally forced the chassis tax to compress. Complete used units that include the 330W GaN brick are the only ones that belong on this board.

Two panels, two comps: **QHD+ 240Hz OLED** vs **dual-mode UHD+/FHD+ mini-LED**. Do not average them. OLED is the one collectors want; mini-LED is the one that survives a bright room. Battery cycle count matters more here than on a Legion — Blade batteries are a known wear item, and a swollen pack in a 0.87" chassis is a $200+ repair plus a warped trackpad. Demand a photo of the bottom case sitting flat.

Hinge, coil whine, and liquid metal. Those three sentences are the Blade 16 legit check. If the seller will not record coil whine at idle and under load, assume it is there and haircut the bid. Look for [Razer Blade 16 RZ09-0510 RTX 4080 with 330W charger](https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw=%22Razer+Blade+16%22+%28RZ09-0510%2C+RZ09-0483%29+4080+-parts+-broken+-swollen) — RZ09-0483 is the 2023 dual-mode 16, a valid 4080, different panel and a slightly softer bid.

## What we left off the rail

- **Alienware m16 R1** — real dump pricing (Dell had Ryzen 9 4080 configs near $1,799 vs ~$2,799 list), but chunky, short battery, and the resale bid is thinner than Legion. Fine to play, not an index.
- **MSI Raider 18 HX** — 18" QHD+/UHD+ monster. Original list was desktop money. Liquidity is worse; you sit on it.
- **Scar 18 / Blade 18** — same GPU, travel-hostile, smaller buyer pool.
- **4090 laptops at 4080 money** — only if TGP and 16GB VRAM are proven. A throttled 4090 is a 4080 with a higher ask.

## Condition sheet before you BIN

1. Photo of the **charger wattage** (330W class for Scar/Legion/Vector/Blade 4080; Zephyrus GaN as specified).
2. **GPU-Z or NVIDIA app** showing RTX 4080 Laptop, 12GB, and TGP.
3. Keyboard close-up, hinge at 90°, bottom case flat on a table.
4. Battery report (powercfg /batteryreport) or at least cycle count.
5. 30-minute stress or a Time Spy screenshot with the laptop model in the same album.

If they will not shoot those, it is not under-retail. It is unverified. Leave it off the wall.
